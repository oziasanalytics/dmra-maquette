Our Procedure

Controls Before, During and After Fieldwork

Every assignment follows the same structured procedure to protect the reliability of the data collected.

01

Pre-Fieldwork

  • Selection of data collectors
  • Interviewer training for each study
  • Simulation of interviews
02

Fieldwork

  • Interviewer assistance by a supervisor
  • Back-check: all interviews are back-checked
03

Post-Fieldwork

  • Back-check control: 25% of the work done by each collector
  • Data entry: coding and processing control
Professional Standards

DMRA fully implements the ESOMAR Code of Conduct (European Society for Opinion and Marketing Research) in all our markets.
