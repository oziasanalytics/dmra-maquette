Start With a Research Brief — or Simply Start With the Question.

Whether you already have a complete research protocol or are still defining the right approach, DMRA can help you determine how best to move your research forward. Tell us what you need to understand, where the research needs to take place and what stage your project has reached.

I Have a Research Brief

Already Know What You Need?

Send us the information available about your project and our team will review your requirements.

Your Research Brief Can Include:

  • Research Objectives — what do you need the research to understand or answer?
  • Market(s) — where will the research take place?
  • Target Population — who do you need to reach?
  • Methodology — quantitative, qualitative, mixed-method — if already defined.
  • Scope & Sample — expected sample size, geographic coverage — where available.
  • Timing — expected fieldwork period, milestones or delivery deadline.

Not everything needs to be finalized before you contact us.
