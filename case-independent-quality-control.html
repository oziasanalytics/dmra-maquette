Côte d'Ivoire & Senegal

Overview

For this assignment, DMRA provided an independent layer of quality control across research conducted in Côte d'Ivoire and Senegal. The role was distinct from primary data collection: DMRA's responsibility was to support the independent verification of research execution and data quality.
