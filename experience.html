Social, Behavioral & Public Health Research

Understanding the Human Factors Behind Decisions and Behaviors

Social and public health research often requires more than measuring awareness or behavior. DMRA's experience includes large-scale national research addressing sensitive and complex health-related questions through quantitative and mixed-method approaches.

The research was designed to explore the factors influencing hesitancy toward HPV vaccine adoption, requiring both large-scale quantitative evidence and deeper qualitative exploration. DMRA supported the execution of this large-scale mixed-method research program, demonstrating its capacity to support large respondent volumes, multiple methodologies and sensitive public health topics.

Case Study 03 — Competitive Intelligence ResearchCôte d'Ivoire

Understanding the Competitive Landscape in Life Insurance

The research required a broader view of the competitive environment, market propositions and competitor activity in Côte d'Ivoire's life insurance sector. DMRA used a multi-source competitive intelligence approach — desk research, mystery shopping, semi-structured interviews, expert interviews, digital intelligence and evidence triangulation — demonstrating its ability to move beyond traditional survey research.

Understanding Products, Pricing & Distribution at the Point of Sale

The assignment required structured information from points of sale across multiple Home Care and Personal Care categories in Côte d'Ivoire. DMRA conducted point-of-sale research capturing brands, origin, pricing, packaging, promotions and key category players, complemented by qualitative interviews with point-of-sale owners or managers.
