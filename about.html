About DMRA

Local Research Expertise Built on International Research Experience

DMRA — Data Marketing Research Agency — is an established independent African research company, founded in 2010, whose leadership and team bring strong international research experience. With offices in Côte d'Ivoire, Cameroon and Senegal, DMRA supports organizations seeking to better understand markets, consumers and communities across Francophone Africa.

The team's experience includes delivering research in complex and demanding operating environments, where strong field knowledge, adaptability and disciplined execution are essential. Today, DMRA builds on this collective experience by combining international research practices, deep local understanding and hands-on execution.

Our Institutional Signature

BUILDING ON THE BEST

For DMRA, Building on the Best means drawing on established research standards, accumulated experience and proven practices while adapting them to the realities of each market, assignment and research question. It reflects our commitment to continuous improvement, reliable research execution and practical insights that help organizations make better-informed decisions.

Our Approach

Research That Connects Methodology With Local Reality

Research methodologies may travel across markets. Context does not. Language, culture, social norms, market structures, respondent accessibility and field realities can all influence how research should be designed and executed.

Research Discipline

Structured methodologies, rigorous execution and attention to data quality.

Local Understanding

Knowledge of the markets, populations and environments in which the research takes place.

Practical Partnership

A flexible approach that allows DMRA to work alongside international research teams or directly with organizations requiring complete research support.

Leadership

Built on International Research Experience

Joseph Marcel Epoupè Ekoué

Founder & Managing Director

DMRA is led by Joseph Marcel Epoupè Ekoué, Founder & Managing Director and former General Manager of Nielsen Côte d'Ivoire. His 20+ years of professional experience in the research industry, together with the experience of senior members of DMRA's team who also built their careers within Nielsen's Côte d'Ivoire operations, provides the company with a strong foundation in research methodology, field execution, quality standards and local market understanding.

DMRA's team brings extensive field-operations experience in Côte d'Ivoire and across French-speaking West and Central Africa, as well as Madagascar.

Joseph Marcel Epoupè Ekoué is an engineer and holds a degree in Strategic and Operational Marketing from the European Institute of Marketing (Belgium).
