About DMRA

Local Research Expertise Built on International Research Experience

DMRA — Data Marketing Research Agency — is a research and insights company supporting organizations seeking to better understand markets, consumers and communities across Francophone Africa. Established in Côte d'Ivoire, DMRA is built around a core team of experienced research professionals, including former Nielsen professionals who worked together in Côte d'Ivoire and developed extensive experience conducting and managing research in demanding operating environments.

Their experience includes periods of significant disruption in Côte d'Ivoire, where maintaining research operations required strong field knowledge, adaptability, disciplined execution and an ability to operate effectively under challenging conditions. Today, DMRA builds on this collective experience by combining international research practices, deep local understanding and hands-on execution.

Our Institutional Signature

BUILDING ON THE BEST

For DMRA, Building on the Best means drawing on established research standards, accumulated experience and proven practices while adapting them to the realities of each market, assignment and research question. It reflects our commitment to continuous improvement, reliable research execution and practical insights that help organizations make better-informed decisions.

Our Approach

Research That Connects Methodology With Local Reality

Research methodologies may travel across markets. Context does not. Language, culture, social norms, market structures, respondent accessibility and field realities can all influence how research should be designed and executed.

Research Discipline

Structured methodologies, rigorous execution and attention to data quality.

Local Understanding

Knowledge of the markets, populations and environments in which the research takes place.

Practical Partnership

A flexible approach that allows DMRA to work alongside international research teams or directly with organizations requiring complete research support.

Leadership

Built on International Research Experience

Joseph Marcel Epoupè Ekoué

Founder & Managing Director

DMRA is led by Joseph Marcel Epoupè Ekoué, Founder & Managing Director and former General Manager of Nielsen Côte d'Ivoire. His professional experience in the research industry, together with the experience of senior members of DMRA's team who also built their careers within Nielsen's Côte d'Ivoire operations, provides the company with a strong foundation in research methodology, field execution, quality standards and local market understanding.

This experience was developed not only through conventional research assignments, but also through operating in complex and demanding environments, where adaptability, continuity of operations and strong local knowledge were essential. Through DMRA, this collective experience is applied to assignments ranging from market and consumer intelligence to social and behavioral research, public health studies, retail research and independent quality control.

International Research Experience. Local Knowledge. Proven Resilience.
