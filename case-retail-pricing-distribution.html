Understanding Products, Pricing & Distribution at the Point of Sale

Côte d'Ivoire

Context

The assignment required structured information from points of sale across multiple Home Care and Personal Care categories in Côte d'Ivoire.

DMRA's Role

DMRA conducted point-of-sale research capturing brands, origin, pricing, packaging, promotions and key category players, complemented by qualitative interviews with point-of-sale owners or managers.
