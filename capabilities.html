Our Capabilities

Research Capabilities Built Around Your Questions

From Research Questions to Reliable Evidence.

DMRA provides integrated research, insights and fieldwork capabilities to help organizations understand markets, consumers, communities and the social and behavioral factors that shape decisions and outcomes. Our work spans market and consumer research, social and behavioral research, public health and development research, retail and distribution intelligence, and complex fieldwork and data-quality assignments across Francophone Africa.

Market & Consumer Intelligence

Understand the Market. Understand the Consumer. Make Better Decisions.

Markets change. Consumer expectations evolve. Competitive environments become more complex. DMRA helps organizations build a clearer understanding of consumers, brands, markets, competitors, retail channels and distribution dynamics — turning research into evidence that supports better commercial and strategic decisions. Our Market & Consumer Intelligence capabilities are organized around four fundamental business questions.

01

Understand the Consumer

Who are your consumers — and what drives their choices?

We help organizations understand consumer needs, attitudes, behaviors, experiences and decision drivers to identify opportunities and strengthen market strategies.

02

Understand the Brand

How is your brand perceived and performing?

DMRA helps organizations understand how consumers perceive their brands, how those perceptions evolve and how marketing activities influence awareness, consideration and engagement.

03

Understand the Market & Competition

Where are the opportunities — and what is happening around you?

We combine research and multiple sources of market evidence to help organizations understand market structures, competitors, customer propositions and emerging opportunities. Featured capability — Competitive Intelligence: combining desk research, mystery shopping, semi-structured interviews, digital intelligence and evidence triangulation to move beyond isolated information and build a complete understanding of the competitive environment.

Market AssessmentCompetitive IntelligenceMystery ShoppingDigital Intelligence
04

Understand Retail & Distribution

What is really happening where products meet the market?

DMRA helps manufacturers, distributors, investors and other organizations understand retail structure, distribution channels, product presence, pricing, promotions and competitive activity at the point of sale.

01

Understand Behaviors & Perceptions

Fieldwork & Data Quality

Reliable Fieldwork. Quality You Can Trust.

Reliable research depends on more than a well-designed questionnaire. It requires careful preparation, capable field teams, consistent supervision and rigorous quality control throughout the process. DMRA provides quantitative and qualitative fieldwork capabilities for organizations requiring reliable local research execution across Francophone African markets.

01

Fieldwork Preparation

Strong fieldwork starts before the first interview.

DMRA prepares each assignment around the requirements of the research methodology, target population and local field environment.

Respondent RecruitmentInterviewer TrainingField Simulations
02

Quantitative & Qualitative Fieldwork

Reliable execution at scale — and beyond the numbers.

DMRA conducts quantitative fieldwork (CAPI, CATI, face-to-face, community-based surveys) and qualitative fieldwork (in-depth interviews, focus groups, triads, expert interviews), delivered independently or integrated into mixed-method designs.

CAPICATIIn-Depth InterviewsFocus Groups
03

Field Supervision & Quality Control

Quality is managed throughout fieldwork — not checked only at the end.

DMRA integrates supervision and quality-control procedures throughout research execution to help identify inconsistencies early and protect data integrity.

Field SupervisionBack-CheckingData Validation
04

Independent Quality Control

An additional layer of confidence in research execution.

DMRA can also operate independently from the primary fieldwork team to provide an additional level of verification.

Proven Experience

Independent quality-control support covering 7,800 interviews across Côte d'Ivoire (6,100) and Senegal (1,700).

Independent QCFieldwork Verification

How We Deliver Research

A Structured Approach from Research Question to Actionable Insight

Every research assignment is different, but reliable research requires a disciplined process. DMRA adapts its approach to the objectives, methodology, target population and local context of each assignment.

01 — Understand the Question

Start with the decision the research needs to support.

02 — Design & Adapt

Build the right methodology for the question and the context.

03 — Prepare the Fieldwork

Turn the methodology into an executable field plan.

04 — Execute the Research

Deliver consistent quantitative and qualitative fieldwork.

05 — Supervise & Protect Quality

Quality is managed throughout fieldwork.

06 — Validate the Data

Move from collected information to reliable evidence.

07 — Analyze & Translate

Research becomes valuable when it answers the original question.
